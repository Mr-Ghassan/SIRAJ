01 · Introduction

One brand universe

This document defines SIRAJ Brand Identity v1.0: the official visual identity, design system and motion system of SIRAJ Real Estate Development. It is written for designers, developers, marketing teams, video teams and external agencies.

Everything shown here exists as a production file in the brand repository. Where this document shows an example, the repository contains the editable source and the exports.

How to use it. Read the foundations first: idea, logo, colour, typography and silk. Then use the application chapters as references. When in doubt, choose restraint.

Source of truth. github.com/Mr-Ghassan/SIRAJ. Tokens, fonts, logos and templates are versioned there.

Silk Reveal: the signature campaign gesture (concept visual).

05 · Brand idea

The lamp sets the standard. Silk carries it. Architecture makes it last.

Light · the Lumen ◆

Siraj (سِراج) means the lamp: one steady light against which everything is seen. The two stone diamonds above the I and the J are its flames, and they mark the standard.

Silk · the philosophy

Soft yet strong, flexible yet cohesive, simple in material and rich in presence, changing with light. Flexibility in thinking, precision in execution, fluidity in experience.

Architecture · lasting value

The grid, square corners, real buildings and generous space express permanence and value. Architecture always comes first.

SIRAJ — Architecture shaped with the elegance, continuity and controlled movement of silk.

07 · Language policy

SIRAJ speaks Arabic

كل منشورات وتسويق سراج، للشركة والمشروع الأول، باللغة العربية فقط.

All communications for SIRAJ and the Founding Project «سراج» are in Arabic only: social, advertising, print, presentations, property marketing, video, signage, events and web.

  • Arabic copy, Tajawal type, right-to-left layouts.
  • Latin is allowed only for the SIRAJ wordmark, website, handle, email and phone. Digits are always Western (0–9).
  • Business card, quotation, tax invoice, receipt and contract cover may stay bilingual, with Arabic primary.
  • No English posts, captions, videos or brochures.
  • English is dormant. It is activated only for a freehold project open to foreign ownership, and only for that project. Archive: 99-Archive/English-Dormant.

11 · SIRAJ Silk

Silk is a philosophy and a behaviour

Soft yet strong. Flexible yet cohesive. Simple in material, rich in presence, changing with light and movement. Silk is SIRAJ's character: flexibility in thinking, precision in execution, fluidity in experience.

RevealSilk moves away to uncover architecture or content.
FrameTwo silk forms or a Silk Corner frame the subject.
ConnectA single ribbon or line links pages, scenes and campaigns.
GuideSilk travels along the 51° axis and leads the eye.
TransitionSilk carries one scene into the next.

Hierarchy on every layout: architecture first, information second, silk as the signature gesture, logo as the final identifier.

Baseline is shared by all letters; the A apex sets the cap line.

20 · Gradients

Light travelling across silk

  • The light source is always at the upper end (51° Silk Axis).
  • Use at most 3 hues, all from the maroon family.
  • Move gradients only as light travelling: a 10–15% stop shift over 2 s or more.
  • No rainbow or SaaS gradients, gold or metallics, gradients in the logo, or gradients behind small text.

27 · Silk rules

How much, where, how

  • Use real silk only: photographed fabric or filmed footage, never a graphic or CG render.
  • Use one silk gesture per layout.
  • Keep silk to 35% of any layout that shows architecture or information.
  • Enter from the lower start and leave toward the upper end.
  • Light comes from a single warm source at the upper end.
  • Keep it satin and maroon, either architectural or precious in scale.
  • Show no more than three dominant folds.
  • Never hide the property.
  • Never let silk touch, cross or frame the logo.
  • Never use silk as a fashion or garment reference, with bodies or mannequins.
  • Never use glossy plastic, metallic, iridescent or non-maroon silk.
  • Never crumple silk into chaotic flutter.
  • Never cover every layout with silk: restraint is the luxury.

43 · Motion principles

The controlled movement of silk

FLOWMovement enters with continuous momentum.
DRAPEElements settle like falling fabric.
REVEALContent is uncovered as silk reveals architecture.
WRAPA ribbon travels around an object or title.
FOLDTransitions behave like controlled folds.
GLIDECamera and UI moves are smooth and confident.
SETTLEEvery animation ends calmly and precisely.

Elegant, cinematic, architectural and controlled. No bounce, no elastic movement, no flashy transitions.

Stagger: 40, 80 or 140 ms. Image settle: scale 1.04 → 1.00, never above 1.08. Parallax: 12%.

Reduced motion: durations collapse to 1 ms and end states are applied directly.

51 · Governance

Fixed, flexible, prohibited

Fixed

  • Official logo and its geometry
  • Core colours: Maroon, Deep, Sand, Stone
  • Typography: Jost, Tajawal, Western digits
  • Proportions, clear space, minimum sizes
  • Icon style
  • Key motion behaviour: intro, outro, easings

Flexible

  • Campaign compositions
  • Silk formations, within the silk rules
  • Photography and project imagery
  • Project naming and endorsement
  • Controlled secondary graphics

Prohibited

  • Altering, redrawing or recolouring the logo
  • Gold, metallic or rainbow effects
  • Silk over the logo or the property
  • Arabic-Indic digits
  • Bounce or elastic motion
  • Stock clichés and HDR imagery
  • Third-party fonts in brand communication

Approval. New templates, campaigns and project sub-brands are reviewed by SIRAJ Marketing. Exceptions are logged in CHANGELOG.md. Brand files change only through versioned releases (v1.x for additions, v2.0 for any change to fixed elements).

The official logo is unaltered, with ½ H clear space and above the minimum size
Correct logo variant for the background, with enough contrast

Layout sits on the grid; one silk gesture at most; silk is under 35%
Silk is real fabric (photo or footage), never a graphic
Silk never touches the logo or hides the property
Imagery is architecture-first, warm and graded with the SIRAJ Silk Grade
RTL is mirrored correctly; bidi runs (phones, URLs) are isolated
Motion uses token easings with no bounce; reduced motion is respected
Contact data comes from brand.config and placeholders are replaced
Recognisable as SIRAJ before seeing the logo
