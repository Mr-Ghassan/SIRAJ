  - the website, always written `WWW.SIRAJPROPERTIES.NET`, the Instagram handle `SIRAJPROPERTIES` (always with the Instagram icon, never «@»), email addresses and the phone number (`7771 4404`);

## 2. How the identity expresses the strategy

**Siraj (سِراج)** is the lamp: one steady light against which everything else is seen. That is exactly the strategy. **SIRAJ begins with a standard.** One strong source, and from it a circle of light that widens without dimming.

> **The lamp sets the standard. Silk carries it. Architecture makes it last.**

| Strategy | Identity expression |
|---|---|
| **A standard, not scale** | One official wordmark, one disciplined palette, one signature gesture per layout. Restraint is the proof of standard. |
| **The Founding Project** | The first project is the brand's reference. Its imagery, materials and story set the benchmark for every later project. |
| **Expanding circles** | The **Lumen ◆** (the light on the I and J) is the centre. Concentric rings and the halo in motion express growth from a strong centre. |
| **Silk philosophy**: soft yet strong, flexible yet cohesive, simple material and rich presence, changing with light | **SIRAJ Silk**: maroon silk as a behaviour that reveals, frames, connects and transitions. *Flexibility in thinking, precision in execution, fluidity in experience.* |
| **Architecture of lasting value** | The grid, square corners, real buildings, generous space: architecture first. |
| **Each project a brand, linked to the parent** | Endorsed project brands: each project has its own name, story and expression, always with the **«A SIRAJ development · من تطوير سراج»** endorsement. |
| **Facts over hype** | Typography and data visualisation built for numbers: light numerals, Western digits, honest charts, mandatory disclaimers. |

## 4. Voice: what we say and what we never say

| We rely on | We avoid |
|---|---|
| Facts and numbers | Exaggeration |
| Design and quality of product | «الأفخم على الإطلاق» / "the most luxurious ever" |
| Location | «الفرصة التي لن تتكرر» / "the opportunity that will never come again" |
| Vision and value | Excessive sales language, countdown pressure, exclamation marks |
| Verified investment information | Unsupported investment promises (guaranteed returns, guaranteed appreciation) |

**Rules**

1. Every claim must be supportable: a fact, a number, a drawing, a material or a date.
2. Investment figures are always labelled (projected, illustrative or historical) and carry the disclaimer line.
3. Write Arabic natively. Arabic is not a translation of the English.
4. One idea per message. Let the architecture and the numbers persuade.

**Sample lines**

| English | العربية |
|---|---|
| A residence shaped by light and silence. | مساكن تتشكّل بالضوء والسكينة. |
| Considered in every detail. Built to last. | مدروسة في كل تفاصيلها. مبنية لتدوم. |
| The project that sets the SIRAJ standard. | المشروع الذي يؤسس معيار سراج. |
| Value that lasts. | قيمة تستمر. |

The **brand line** is still under consideration. The official positioning statement stands on its own, and a short line may be adopted later. Candidates, in no particular order:

## 5. Marketing framework: the five value pillars

Every project communication (website, brochure, presentation, film, sales script) is structured around five pillars. The client must understand **within seconds why this project is different.**

| # | Pillar | Question | Typical proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Location / الموقع** | Why here, and why will its value grow? | Map, distances, infrastructure, urban plans |
| 2 | **Product / المنتج** | How is it designed for the client and the market? | Plans, unit mix, specifications, materials |
| 3 | **Experience / التجربة** | How will it feel to live or invest here? | Photography, CGI, film, sales centre |
| 4 | **Investment value / القيمة الاستثمارية** | Why is it a good asset, not just a unit? | Price per m², rent, yield, resale (labelled) |
| 5 | **Trust / الثقة** | Who develops it, and how will it be delivered and managed? | Developer profile, team, programme, progress, escrow |

The pillar icons are `location`, `floor-plan`, `view`, `investment` and `security` (07-Icon-System), always marked with the Lumen.

## 6. Campaign lifecycle: PRE-LAUNCH · LAUNCH · SUSTAIN

| Phase | Goal | Silk behaviour | Typical assets |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PRE-LAUNCH** | Build interest without revealing everything | **Veil**: silk wraps or partially conceals the building; details are withheld | Teaser posts and stories, register-interest landing page, broker kit, PR, project-story film |
| **LAUNCH** | Maximum market focus in a short time | **Reveal**: silk sweeps away (*Silk Reveal*, *Silk Sweep*) | Launch film, event (stage, invitation), outdoor, digital campaign, brochure, price list |
| **SUSTAIN** | Momentum until sell-out and handover | **Connect**: a single ribbon or line links progress stories | Construction updates, buyer testimonials, investment content, remaining units, milestones, handover |

## 7. Brand architecture: endorsed project brands

- **Masterbrand:** SIRAJ (the official wordmark), which stands for the developer, the standard and trust.
- **Project brands:** *each project is treated as a brand of its own within the SIRAJ system.* A project receives a name, a story, a personality and a visual expression (photography, a project typographic mark, and optionally a project accent drawn from the SIRAJ palette).
- **Mandatory link to the parent:** every project brand carries the **endorsement lockup «A SIRAJ development · من تطوير سراج»** (`14-Property-Marketing/00-Endorsement-Lockup/`). The SIRAJ wordmark appears on every project communication, at least on the closing frame or page.
- **What all projects share:** SIRAJ typography, the grid, the Lumen, silk as the reveal gesture, the SIRAJ Silk Grade, and the voice. *SIRAJ projects should not all look alike, but they should all feel like SIRAJ.*
- **The Founding Project:** an **internal** term for the first project in Muscat. It is never used in public communication (client rule 2026-10-12): publicly the project is «سراج» with its city. The strategy lines (core statement, principle, model) are also internal and do not appear in posts or ads.
- **Naming (recommended):** names from the world of light and craft, such as Noor (light), Qandeel (lantern), Mishkat (lamp niche) and Harir (silk). These make every project a chapter of the same story. The names used across this system are **demonstration names** until the real project names are confirmed.

## 9. Growth model and brand implications

**First project → several projects in Muscat → product diversification → development partnerships → expansion within Oman → developing and managing assets for others → selected regional opportunities.**

The identity is built to scale with this model:

1. **Tokens and templates** (09–14) let new projects be produced without diluting the standard.
2. **Endorsed project brands** let the portfolio diversify (residential, mixed-use, commercial, compounds) while still feeling like SIRAJ.
3. **Future service lines** (development management, advisory, investment and asset management, community management) use descriptor lockups such as «SIRAJ · Development Management» set in Jost and Tajawal under the official wordmark. They are introduced only when the capability exists, as the strategy states.

## 10. The recognisability test

> **Would someone recognise this as SIRAJ even before seeing the logo?**

A piece passes when at least three of the five signals below are present, it states something true and specific, and none of the prohibitions are broken.

1. The maroon, stone and ivory palette, with restraint.
2. One silk gesture (veil, reveal, frame or connect).
3. The Lumen ◆ used as a marker of light.
4. Light Jost and Tajawal typography, with generous space and Western digits.
5. Architecture-first imagery graded with the SIRAJ Silk Grade.
