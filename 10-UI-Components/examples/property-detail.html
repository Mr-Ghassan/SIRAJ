Overview

Twenty-two storeys of calm above Al Khuwair.

A slender tower in warm stone and bronze, oriented to catch the morning sea breeze and turn its back to the afternoon sun.

148 residences — from one-bedroom apartments to four full-floor penthouses — sit above a shaded podium of gardens, a 25-metre infinity pool and a residents’ majlis. Every home has a deep loggia facing the Sea of Oman, and is finished in natural oak, Omani limestone and brushed bronze.
