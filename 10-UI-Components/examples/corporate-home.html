About SIRAJ

SIRAJ — the lamp — builds places that hold light. We design with the patience of architecture and the continuity of silk, so a home feels calm on its first day and on its ten-thousandth.

Since 2011 we have delivered more than 2,400 residences across Muscat and the Batinah coast — each one planned around daylight, shade, and the view it frames.

From the first sketch to the handover walk-through, one team stays with every project and every owner.
