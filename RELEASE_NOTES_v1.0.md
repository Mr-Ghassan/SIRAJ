## Change policy

v1.0 is a permanent release. The website and every later digital product consume it as a dependency and do not change it. Any change to the brand foundation ships as a new, separately versioned release (v1.1 / v2.0) with its own notes, QA and tag. The tag `siraj-brand-system-v1.0.0` is never moved or reused.
