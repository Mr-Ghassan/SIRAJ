SIRAJ Design System · Digital

UI Components

The official interface library for SIRAJ Real Estate Development. Architectural, quietly premium and fully bilingual — every component mirrors in Arabic, follows the brand tokens and works in light and dark.

  • Square by defaultArchitectural edges. Inputs and chips may soften to 2 px. No pills.
  • Hairlines over shadowsStructure comes from 1 px Linen rules, not stacked drop shadows.
  • The Lumen marksThe 45° Stone diamond is the bullet, the active state and the step marker.
  • Silk, sparinglyOne Silk Corner on the top-end of a hero or featured card — once or twice per view.

Usage

  • Primary (Maroon) for the single most important action: enquire, reserve, submit.
  • Secondary (outline) for supporting actions; ghost inside toolbars and tables.
  • Directional icons take .sj-icon--dir so they mirror in RTL.
  • Arabic labels drop uppercase and tracking automatically.

Do

  • Use verbs: “Book a viewing”, “Reserve unit”.
  • Keep one primary button per view.
  • Use --light variants on imagery and silk.

Don't

  • Round corners or use pill shapes.
  • Add drop shadows or gradients to buttons.
  • Place two Maroon buttons side by side.

Usage

  • Links in running text use the accent colour (Maroon on light, Sand on dark).
  • aria-pressed turns an icon button into a toggle (save, favourite).
  • Minimum hit area: 36 × 36 px (small), 48 × 48 px default.

Do

  • Pair icon-only buttons with a tooltip on desktop.
  • Use filled icons only to signal an “on” state.

Don't

  • Underline links with the browser default.
  • Use Stone for link text — it fails contrast on Ivory.

Usage

  • Always a visible label — placeholders are hints, not labels.
  • Errors pair colour with an icon and a sentence, and add a 2 px start edge.

Do

  • Write errors that explain the fix.
  • Group related fields on the 12-column grid.

Don't

  • Use floating labels or underline-only inputs.
  • Rely on red alone to signal an error.

  • Pre-select the most likely option.
  • Keep option labels short; push detail into meta.

Don't

  • Use a dropdown for two or three options — use a segmented control.
05

Checkbox, radio & switch

The radio is a Lumen — a diamond that fills when chosen. Checkboxes are square with a 2 px radius; the switch is a rectangular track with a square knob.

Amenities
Preferred contact
Notifications 
<label class="sj-check"><input type="checkbox" checked> Sea view</label>
<label class="sj-radio"><input type="radio" name="contact" checked> Phone call</label>
<label class="sj-switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch"> Payment reminders</label>

Usage

  • Native inputs, restyled — keyboard and screen-reader behaviour comes for free.
  • Switches apply immediately; checkboxes are submitted with a form.
  • The switch knob travels with inset-inline-start, so it mirrors in RTL.

Do

  • Group with a fieldset and visible legend.

Don't

  • Replace the diamond radio with a circle — it is a brand signature.
