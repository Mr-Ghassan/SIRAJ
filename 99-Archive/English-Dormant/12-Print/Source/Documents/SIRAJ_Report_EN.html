At {{demo_projects.noor.en}} the reinforced-concrete core reached level 24 in August, two weeks ahead of the baseline. Façade mock-up panels were approved in September and production has started off-site.

{{demo_projects.harir.en}} completed infrastructure for the first cluster of fourteen villas. Pool shells and boundary walls follow in the next quarter.

The {{demo_projects.mishkat.en}} façade is under design review after a supplier change; the revised programme protects the {{demo_projects.mishkat.handover}} handover.

{{demo_projects.qandeel.en}} began enabling works and utility diversions. Main-contract tenders close in November.

Next quarter
Topping-out ceremony at {{demo_projects.noor.en}}; first villa handover rehearsal at {{demo_projects.harir.en}}; award of the {{demo_projects.qandeel.en}} main contract.
Risk to watch
Façade supply for {{demo_projects.mishkat.en}}. Mitigation: dual sourcing of glazing units agreed with the contractor.
