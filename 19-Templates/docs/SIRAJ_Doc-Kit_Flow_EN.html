Client site visit guidelines

How we welcome clients to an active construction site: safely, calmly and with the same care they will find in the finished home.

01Purpose

Site visits let clients see their future home take shape. They are also the moment when the promise of a SIRAJ residence meets the reality of a building site. These guidelines set out how visits are planned, hosted and closed, so that every visit is safe for the client and consistent with the experience in our sales centres.

The guidelines apply to every visit by a client, a client's representative or a prospective buyer to {{demo_projects.noor.en}}, {{demo_projects.harir.en}}, {{demo_projects.qandeel.en}} and {{demo_projects.mishkat.en}} while construction is in progress.

02Planning a visit

Visits are booked at least five working days in advance through the CRM, so the site team can prepare a safe route and pause noisy work where possible.

  • Visits run on Sundays to Thursdays, between 08:30 and 11:30, outside concrete pours and crane lifts.
  • A group is limited to six guests and two SIRAJ hosts; children under 16 do not enter the site.
  • The site manager confirms the route, the viewing points and the welfare facilities 24 hours before the visit.
  • The client receives a confirmation with the meeting point, parking, dress guidance and the host's mobile number.

03Safety

Safety is never traded for convenience. Every guest is inducted, equipped and accompanied for the whole visit.

  1. Guests sign the visitor register and receive a short safety induction at the meeting point.
  2. Helmets, high-visibility vests, safety glasses and overshoes are provided in guest sizes and in the SIRAJ colours.
  3. Guests stay on the marked route and never enter areas behind barriers, at open edges or under suspended loads.
  4. The visit stops if the host or the site manager judges conditions unsafe, including heat above 40 °C or wind above 38 km/h.
  5. In an emergency, guests follow their host to the assembly point shown on the induction card.
Heat
From May to September, visits finish by 10:00. Water and shaded rest are offered at every viewing point.

04Hosting

A visit should feel like an appointment, not a tour. The host introduces the site manager, explains what the client is seeing and what will change by the next visit, and answers questions with the same care as in the sales centre.

Hosts carry the floor plans of the client's residence and a tablet with the latest progress photographs. Photography by clients is welcome on the route; images of workers' faces are not shared.

After the visit

  • Return the equipment and close the visitor register.
  • Send a thank-you message within 24 hours with two photographs of the residence's floor.
  • Record the visit and any questions in the CRM, and flag finish or layout queries to Design Management.
