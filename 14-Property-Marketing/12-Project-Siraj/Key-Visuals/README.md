# Project «سراج» · Silk & Light key visuals (v1.0, 2026-10-12)

Hero posts built on the brand idea: سراج is the lamp. The building glows from within, real maroon silk veils it, reveals it or flies around it, and warm light gives the drama. Shots use cinematic angles.

| Key visual | Scene | Display line (Tajawal 200 ↔ 800) |
|---|---|---|
| **Veil** | Blue hour; giant silk draped over the building, light glowing through it; a shaft of light | «أوّلُ / الضوء.» (pre-launch teaser) |
| **Ribbon** | Drone view at dusk over Muscat and the Hajar mountains; one silk ribbon sweeping around the building | «حين يحلّ المساء، / يُضيء سراج.» |
| **Lantern** | Night, telephoto across a wet street; the building as a lantern with a column of light; a silk ribbon in the foreground | «في قلب المدينة، / بيتٌ من ضوء.» |
| **Reveal** | Cinematic cloudy day; the silk slides off and the sun breaks through | «حين يُرفع الستار، / يتّضح كل شيء.» (light theme) |
| **Detail** | Close-up: light through the perforated terracotta screens, the edge of silk | «مدروسة / في كل تفاصيلها.» |

Each key visual comes in two formats:
- Post 1080×1350
- Story 1080×1920

Both are rendered at 2× (`SIRAJ_Project-Siraj_KV_<Name>_<Post|Story>_AR_v1.0.png`).

**Copy:** depth without pretension. The copy speaks about light, place, material and life, never about SIRAJ's strategy. «المشروع المؤسِّس» is internal and never appears.

**Contact line:** phone **7771 4404** + the Instagram icon and **SIRAJPROPERTIES** only (never «@»), never the website (client rules 2026-10-12/13). Values come from `00-Brand-Foundation/brand.config.json → contact`. Rebuild with `node tools/build-social.js` (refreshes the brand data), then `node tools/build-project-siraj-kv.js`.

**Sources:** the photos are in `../Source/KeyVisuals/` (4:5 and 9:16). They were generated with Google Nano Banana Pro (`gemini-3-pro-image`, 4K) via ElevenLabs, using the publish-ready renders in `../Source/Clean/` as references so the architecture is the project's own. The Veil, Lantern and Reveal stories were outpainted to 9:16 from the chosen 4:5 frame; the Lantern and Reveal posts are 4:5 crops of that extension, which leaves more sky for the copy.
