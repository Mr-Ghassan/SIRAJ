**What changed against the architect's renders**
- **People rule:** every person (and the cyclist's bicycle) is removed.
- **Signage:** the placeholder «SIGNAGE» lettering is replaced by blank, softly back-lit fascia bands. These are ready for the real tenant or project signage.
- **Light:** day views have a cinematic overcast sky with a warm break of light from the upper right; night views are blue hour with warm interiors and uplit terracotta screens. Moon glare was removed. The side night view went from 1122 px to 4K.
- **Architecture unchanged:** massing, floors, openings, screens, planters and materials match the originals. Checked with an edge-alignment test (84–92 % of the original edges kept for the building views; lower on the corner day view because of the new sky). The perforation pattern in the terracotta screens is re-rendered, not redrawn by hand.

**How they were made:** AI edit with Google Nano Banana Pro (`gemini-3-pro-image`, 4K, 4:5) via ElevenLabs Creative, flow `ivykkqWIKIV2freA5NgY`. Four variations per view; the most faithful was chosen.
