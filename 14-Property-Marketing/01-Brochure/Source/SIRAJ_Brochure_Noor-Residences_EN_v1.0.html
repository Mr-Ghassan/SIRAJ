01 · Vision

Light, given a place to live.

Noor rises from Al Khuwair as a single, quiet gesture: twenty-four floors of travertine fins that catch the morning sun from the sea and hold the evening glow of the mountains.

Noor Residences, Al Khuwair · artist's impression
02 · Architecture

Drawn upward,
in stone and shade.

The façade is a rhythm of vertical travertine fins, spaced to shade every window from the high sun while opening each residence to long views of the Gulf of Oman. Deep reveals give the tower its depth; glass is set back, never exposed.

Inside, the plan is generous and calm: wide frontages, ceilings of 3.2 metres, and a single private lift lobby for every eight residences.
