## 1. The name

**Siraj (سِراج)** is the lamp: a source of steady light. The light is already present in the official wordmark. The two stone-coloured diamonds above the **I** and the **J** are flames of light set on architectural stems.

The brand turns this into a simple, ownable story:

> **The lamp gives light. Silk carries it. Architecture holds it.**

---

## 2. Brand idea

> **SIRAJ — Architecture shaped with the elegance, continuity and controlled movement of silk.**

Silk is the brand's **behaviour**, not its decoration. It reveals, frames, connects and moves on. It catches light the way good architecture does. It is soft and strong at once, and it turns a simple surface into something memorable.

The idea has three parts:

| | Role | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| **Light (the Lumen ◆)** | Purpose: clarity, warmth, guidance | The diamond from the wordmark, used as marker, pin, bullet and active state |
| **Silk (the gesture)** | Personality: refinement, continuity, craft | Maroon silk ribbon, drape, fold and line, and motion that flows and settles |
| **Architecture (the order)** | Substance: permanence, value, precision | The grid, square corners, real buildings, generous space |

## 3. Positioning

**For** buyers, investors and partners in Oman and the region who expect more than square metres,
**SIRAJ is** a real estate developer that creates calm, precisely crafted places of lasting value,
**because** every project is shaped with the same discipline as the brand itself: architecture first, detail always, and nothing loud.

**Territory:** Premium Real Estate × Contemporary Architecture × Luxury Materiality × Modern Corporate Precision.

**SIRAJ is quietly premium rather than loudly luxurious.**

### SIRAJ is / is not

| SIRAJ is | SIRAJ is not |
|---|---|
| Calm, confident, precise | Loud, boastful, flashy |
| Architectural and material | Decorative or ornamental |
| Warm maroon, stone and ivory | Generic gold-and-marble luxury |
| Modern Omani, regionally fluent | Cliché "GCC luxury" or imported fashion |
| Corporate credibility with craft | A fashion or lifestyle brand |
| One signature gesture per layout | Silk everywhere |

## 5. Values

1. **Precision.** We draw it right, build it right and say it right.
2. **Restraint.** We use one gesture, not ten. Space and silence are part of the design.
3. **Continuity.** Like silk, our work connects: from first sketch to handover and beyond.
4. **Light.** We design for daylight, clarity and openness, in buildings and in communication.
5. **Permanence.** We create value that lasts longer than a campaign.

## 6. Personality and voice

The **personality** is a seasoned architect who is also a gracious host: knowledgeable, calm, warm and never in a hurry.

| Voice principle | Do | Don't |

## 7. The recognisability test

Every piece is checked against one question:

> **Would someone recognise this as SIRAJ even before seeing the logo?**

A piece passes when at least three of the five signals below are present and none of the prohibitions in the guidelines are broken.

1. The maroon, stone and ivory palette, used with restraint.
2. One silk gesture (ribbon, drape, fold, line, Silk Corner) that reveals, frames or connects.
3. The Lumen ◆ used as a marker or indicator.
4. Light Jost and Tajawal typography with tracked uppercase eyebrows and generous space.
5. Real, warm, architecture-first imagery graded with the SIRAJ Silk Grade.

## 8. Brand architecture

- **Masterbrand:** SIRAJ (the official wordmark).
- **Projects:** named developments (demo names in this system: *Noor Residences*, *Harir Villas*, *Qandeel Square*, *Mishkat Business Centre*). They are set in Jost Light or Tajawal Light with the **"A SIRAJ development · من تطوير سراج"** endorsement. Projects do not get their own logos unless the governance board approves one; even then the endorsement remains.
- **Naming family (recommended):** names from the world of light and craft, such as Noor (light), Qandeel (lantern), Mishkat (lamp niche) and Harir (silk). These make every project a chapter of the same story.
